Batchbook privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What Batchbook stores

Your recipes, prices, price book, shop presets and business details are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. Batchbook has no accounts and no server that receives your data.

What Batchbook sends

Batchbook does not collect analytics, use advertising identifiers or share data with third parties. If you buy Batchbook Pro, the purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement. Batchbook only asks the Store whether your Microsoft account owns Pro.

Removing your data

Delete recipes inside the app, or uninstall Batchbook to remove everything it stored on your device.
